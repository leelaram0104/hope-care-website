About Us

Who We Are

A caring place where children and senior citizens can live with safety, dignity and hope.

Our Story

Hope & Care Foundation was created around a simple belief: everyone deserves a safe place, caring relationships and an opportunity to live a meaningful life. Our work focuses on day-to-day care, education, healthcare, nutrition and emotional support.


We work together with volunteers, donors and the wider community to create a warm, family-like environment.

Our Mission

To provide dependable care and practical support for children and senior citizens while protecting their dignity and encouraging their growth, wellbeing and independence.

  • Safe and respectful living environment
  • Education and personal development
  • Healthcare and nutrition
  • Community participation
Our Values

What Guides Our Work

Compassion

We listen, understand and care.

Dignity

Every person is treated with respect.

Community

We grow stronger by helping together.
